About

Abhinav Gaurav

Founder, Antara Innovations. Author, Pixelated Souls. Cyberpsychologist working hands-on in AI strategy and governance.

The territory

Abhinav's work sits where AI, human behaviour and governance meet, a combination that most AI commentary treats as three separate conversations. He writes and advises from the position that the interesting risk in AI was never really about the model. It's about what happens to human judgement once the model is trusted to carry more of it.

That lens comes from a background most people in AI strategy don't have: training in cyberpsychology, sitting alongside years of hands-on enterprise AI and transformation work in financial services and technology.

The work

Founder of Antara Innovations and Antara Wellness, the psychological and psychosomatic wellbeing app built on the same underlying conviction, that people change when they feel understood, not analysed.

Author of Pixelated Souls. Creator of the Keel, Sentinel, Soundings and HALO frameworks. Writer of The Awakened Intelligence, a weekly newsletter for banking, insurance and risk leaders on how AI changes judgement, and what to do about it.
