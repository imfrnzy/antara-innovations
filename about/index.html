The territory

Where technology meets human judgement

Abhinav’s work sits at the intersection of AI, human behaviour and psychology. Rather than treating these as separate disciplines, he examines what happens when they collide inside real organisations.

The central question is not simply what an intelligent system can do. It is whether people understand what it is doing, know when to trust it, and remain accountable when intelligence becomes increasingly automated.

The perspective

How technology changes human behaviour

AI is often discussed as a technology problem: models, data, infrastructure, security and deployment. Abhinav’s work looks at the other side of the equation.

How does technology change judgement?

How does repeated reliance on intelligent systems change behaviour?

What happens to trust, responsibility and decision-making when the machine becomes part of the cognitive loop?
