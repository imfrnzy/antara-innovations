Frameworks

Frameworks for AI governance, security and leadership. Built from real-world AI incidents and use cases.

Governance operating model

Keel

Govern First.

The operating model for building and governing an AI Centre of Excellence.

Keel helps you decide what to build, what to stop, which use cases get funded, how they are checked before deployment, and how people are trained.

The principle is simple: the foundation stays consistent whether the organisation has three employees or thirty thousand.

AI security

Sentinel

Secure your AI.

Six links form one security chain: identity, authority, context, action, evidence and adaptation.

Traditional access control asks who can access and approve. Sentinel asks what an agent can see, what it can change, and whether its actions can be reconstructed.

The result is an agent inventory, an action-risk register and an evidence architecture, without requiring new access or integrations.

Diagnostic

Soundings

Know what’s running.

Soundings is a rapid diagnostic that maps the AI use cases operating inside an organisation and tests whether the oversight around each one matches its real risk.

Designed to expose the gap between what organisations think is governed and what is actually live.

Leadership operating model

HALO

Human-AI Leadership Operating Model.

When machines become intelligent, HALO keeps humans leading the loop through clarity, acknowledgement and decision transparency.

The objective is simple: preserve trust as AI increases the speed and scale of work.

How they fit together

One system, four lenses.

Soundings finds what AI is actually running, who knows about it, and whether the oversight matches its real risk.

Sentinel secures what AI agents can see, change and do, and whether their actions can be reconstructed afterwards.

Keel governs how AI is selected, built, deployed and scaled, with the controls needed to keep adoption responsible.

HALO keeps humans leading the AI loop, with judgement, trust and accountability at the centre of every decision.

Together, they connect discovery, security, governance and human judgement.
