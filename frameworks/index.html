Governance operating model

Keel

Govern First.

The operating model for building and governing an AI Centre of Excellence.

Keel helps you decide what to build, what to stop, which use cases get funded, how they are checked before deployment, and how people are trained.

The principle is simple: the foundation stays consistent whether the organisation has three employees or thirty thousand.

AI security

Sentinel

Secure your AI.

Six links form one security chain: identity, authority, context, action, evidence and adaptation.

Traditional access control asks who can access and approve. Sentinel asks what an agent can see, what it can change, and whether its actions can be reconstructed.

The result is an agent inventory, an action-risk register and an evidence architecture, without requiring new access or integrations.

Soundings

Know what’s running.

Soundings is a rapid diagnostic that maps the AI use cases operating inside an organisation and tests whether the oversight around each one matches its real risk.

How they fit together

One system, three parts.

Keel sets the policy. The six-link chain is where the actual work happens, and where Sentinel's questions apply. Soundings watches the whole thing and feeds what it sees back into stronger controls.

↓ Governance over the chain ↓

01

Identity

Who is acting?

  • Human user
  • AI agent
  • Shared or delegated identity

Establish and verify identity for every action.

02

Authority

What is it authorised to do?

  • Task-level authorisation
  • Least privilege by design
  • Approval workflows

Grant the minimum authority required.

03

Context

What does it use?

  • Data sources and access
  • External information
  • Trust and data quality

Control what informs its decisions, and trust it.

04

Action & Consequence

What can it cause?

  • Read, write, execute
  • Ability to trigger other agents
  • Human in the loop, where needed

Restrict and monitor real-world impact.

05

Evidence

Can we reconstruct it?

  • End-to-end audit logs
  • Decision inputs and outputs
  • Tamper-resistant records

Prove what happened, and why.

06

Adaptation

What should change?

  • Incident and near-miss learning
  • Control recommendations
  • Evolving threat landscape

Learn from what happened and propose changes.

↑ Learning feedback loop: insights from evidence and adaptation inform stronger controls ↑

HALO sits with Keel, at the human end, keeping judgement and accountability with a person even as the chain runs faster than any person could watch alone.
