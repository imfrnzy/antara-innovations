Frameworks

Governance operating model

Keel

Originally drafted as Govern First.

The operating model for building and governing an AI Centre of Excellence.

Keel defines what an organisation should build, what it should not, how use cases are selected and resourced, how they are checked before deployment, and how people are trained to use AI properly rather than work around it.

The principle is simple: the foundation stays consistent whether the organisation has three employees or thirty thousand. What changes is the depth of control.

AI security

Sentinel

Securing AI agents from access to consequence.

Traditional access control asks who has access and who approved it. Sentinel asks the harder questions: what an agent can see, what it can change, whether its actions can be reconstructed, and what happens when the system learns or changes.

Six links form one security chain: identity, authority, context, action, evidence and adaptation.

The result is an agent inventory, an action-risk register and an evidence architecture — without requiring new access or integrations.

Diagnostic

Soundings

Find out what AI is actually running before you decide what needs governing.

Soundings is a rapid diagnostic that maps the AI use cases operating inside an organisation and tests whether the oversight around each one matches its real risk.

It is designed for the uncomfortable gap between what an organisation believes is governed and what is actually live.

Leadership operating model

HALO

Human-AI Leadership Operating Model.

AI can raise the intelligence of the workplace without improving the quality of its human relationships.

HALO makes clarity, acknowledgement, decision transparency and rapid repair part of everyday leadership — not another culture programme.

The objective is simple: preserve trust while the organisation increases the speed and scale of AI-enabled work.

How they fit together

One system, four lenses.

Soundings finds what is actually running and who knows about it.

Sentinel secures what those agents can see, change and do.

Keel provides the operating model for governing AI as the programme scales.

HALO addresses the human leadership layer — where judgement, trust and accountability determine whether the system works in practice.

Together, they connect discovery, security, governance and human judgement.
