The signal most organisations miss

"I feel tense most days. Like my body doesn't properly switch off." Fine on the surface. Not fine underneath.

It isn't a workload problem. People aren't only responding to volume, they're responding to uncertainty, to not knowing whether anyone actually notices them. The brain runs a quiet background check, constantly: am I safe here, do I matter. When that check gets no answer, the body answers anyway, through tension, irritability, and a tiredness no amount of sleep fixes.

IQ is rising. EQ is dropping.

What AI is actively raising

  • Speed and analytical throughput
  • Information processing at scale
  • Decisional efficiency
  • Output volume per person

What AI is quietly eroding

  • Human signal clarity
  • The felt sense of being seen
  • Decision transparency
  • Trust repair speed

AI isn't the problem. The gap between what it delivers and what leaders still owe people, that's the problem.

Why this moment is different

AI shifts what "good" looks like

People no longer compare themselves only to colleagues. They compare themselves to machines that don't tire, don't doubt, don't ask for context. That changes what people fear, not just what they do.

AI compresses the signals of status

You used to know where you stood by who asked your opinion. AI reduces those moments, fewer people needed in the room, less ambient feedback. When signals get rarer, silence gets louder.

AI narrows the space to be human

Errors get flagged faster, gaps become visible quicker. In a workplace where safety was already thin, that compresses it further, and people stop trying new things.

We govern the technology. We don't govern the atmosphere it creates. That's the gap.

Clarity and Acknowledgement, in detail

Clarity

Uncertainty activates the brain's threat detection. It can't distinguish organisational ambiguity from physical danger. Minimum: no major decision without a context statement, no silence exceeding 48 hours after something goes wrong.

Acknowledgement

The brain runs a constant check, do I matter here. No answer defaults to no, and that drives withdrawal. Minimum: a response within 24 hours to anything brought to you, not a solution, just a signal it landed.

Decision Transparency and Quick Repair, in detail

Decision Transparency

Side-channel decisions erode trust faster than bad decisions. The brain struggles with unexplained exclusion. Minimum: a brief rationale for any decision affecting someone's work, even "this was above my level."

Quick Repair

Rupture is normal in every relationship, it's the unrepaired ones that do lasting damage. Minimum: a 48-hour repair window. Doesn't need to be perfect, needs to happen.

The floor, not the ceiling

Five minimum human standards. Non-negotiable. Below these, HALO cannot function.

01

No silence longer than 48 hours after a concern is raised. Even a brief acknowledgement counts. Silence reads as dismissal.

02

No decision affecting someone's work without a brief rationale. "Here's why" is usually three sentences.

03

No performance feedback delivered only through process. A form is not feedback, there must be a real conversation.

04

AI-generated communication must not replace human acknowledgement for anything sensitive. Efficiency isn't the point when someone's wellbeing is at stake.

05

Every person gets one agenda-free check-in a week. Not "how's the work," just "how are you, actually." Four minutes.
