The territory

AI governance, minus the theatre

Most AI governance stops at what a system is allowed to access. Antara Innovations' work starts from what it can actually cause, whether that can be reconstructed afterwards, and whether the person meant to be supervising it still knows when not to trust it. That's the thread running through the frameworks, the newsletter and the wellbeing work: technology examined for what it does to human judgement, not just what it can do.
