That is the thread running through the frameworks and the wellbeing work alike: technology examined for what it does to human judgement, not just what it can do.

What does Antara Innovations do?

Antara Innovations is a specialised AI advisory working on AI security, governance and human-AI leadership, alongside Antara Wellness, a science-led psychological and psychosomatic wellbeing product.

Which markets does Antara Innovations work with?

Antara Innovations is based in Zurich, Switzerland, with roots in the UK, and works with organisations across the UK, Switzerland, India and beyond.

What frameworks has Antara Innovations built?

Four proprietary frameworks: Keel, for AI governance and Centre of Excellence operating models; Sentinel, for AI agent security; Soundings, a diagnostic for what AI is actually running inside an organisation; and HALO, a human-AI leadership operating model.

Who founded Antara Innovations?

Antara Innovations was founded by Abhinav Gaurav, a cyberpsychologist and AI strategist, and author of Pixelated Souls.
