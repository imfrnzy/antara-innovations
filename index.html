    The thinking behind it

    Most AI governance stops at what a system is allowed to access. We start from what it can actually cause.

    Whether that can be reconstructed afterwards, and whether the person meant to be supervising it still knows when not to trust it. That is the thread through every framework here, and through the wellbeing work: technology examined for what it does to human judgement, not just what it can do.

    Antara Innovations deploys governed AI agents, backed by advisory work, on AI security, governance and human-AI leadership, alongside Antara Wellness, a science-led psychological and psychosomatic wellbeing product.

    Four proprietary frameworks: Keel, for AI governance and Centre of Excellence operating models; Sentinel, for AI agent security; Soundings, a diagnostic for what AI is actually running inside an organisation; and HALO, a human-AI leadership operating model.
