Find out what shape your AI programme should actually take.

Not whether to use AI. Whether one team should build it, whether business units should build their own, or something in between, and whether your organisation is actually ready to start either way.

This isn't a maturity score. It's a short conversation, the same questions we'd ask in the first week of standing up a governed AI function, scored against a fixed rule, then written up properly, not just handed back as a chart.

The centre that never lets go

Centralised was meant to be scaffolding while trust was being built. Eighteen months later it's a permanent department, and every team tired of waiting has built something of its own nobody in the centre can see.

Governance nobody enforces

Federated works if the light-touch centre is actually reviewing the highest-risk work. Without that, it isn't federated, it's just ungoverned with a nicer name.

The model that hides the real question

Hybrid looks like the safe middle option. Run without an explicit rule for which work goes where, it quietly becomes an excuse never to decide.

Three different failure modes, one shared cause: the operating model was never actually decided, just assumed.
