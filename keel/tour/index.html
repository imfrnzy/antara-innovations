KEEL

The keel goes down first. Everything else is built on it.

The operating model is rarely decided. It's assumed.

Nobody sits down and chooses whether one team builds everything, or business units build their own, or something between. It just happens, by default, by whoever moved first. Then eighteen months in, nobody can agree what the CoE was actually meant to be.

How it is held to account

Rules, not vibes

01

One fixed rule at intake

Each use case either ships on its own or needs human review. The same inputs always give the same decision.

02

Gates are checked, not assumed

The agent looks for the owner, the test and the fallback, instead of asking whether they exist.

03

The foundation stays the same

Size, sector and market change which controls are switched on, not the model underneath.

The agent is held to the same questions it asks of everyone else.
