Product · PulseX

An assistant for clinicians who use Cliniko.

PulseX connects to your own Cliniko account and takes the admin off your plate: your day at a glance, who needs what before they arrive, and session notes drafted for you to check.

It suggests. You confirm. Nothing changes in Cliniko without your tap.

Privacy and terms ↓

What it does

Less admin, more attention for the person in front of you

Your day. Today’s diary with what needs attention for each patient: medical alerts, unfinished notes, open invoices, forms, recalls.

Session notes. Record a session and get a draft in your own Cliniko template. It is saved as a draft only, and every point is checked against what was actually said.

Home visits. Real drive times, when to set off, and a warning when a turnaround is too tight.

Quiet reminders. A short note in the morning, a heads-up when a slot frees up, a nudge when a patient arrives. Notifications never carry patient names.

Memory. It learns how you like to work. You can see everything it has learned and delete it.

Good to know

Admin help, not clinical advice

PulseX does not diagnose or decide anything about a patient. Cliniko stays the place where records are kept. PulseX is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Cliniko.

PulseX is a monthly subscription with a 3 day free trial, bought inside the app. Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.
