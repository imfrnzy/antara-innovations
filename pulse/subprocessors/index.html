PulseX: Subprocessors

The companies that handle information for PulseX. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Who handles information for PulseX

These are the companies that handle information on our behalf (our "subprocessors"). Clinics can use this list for their own checks. We will tell you in the app or by email at least 30 days before we add a new one.

CompanyWhat it does for PulseXWherePatient information
SupabaseDatabase, sign-in, our server functions and the emails that carry sign-in codesIrelandYour account details, held appointments and their notes, and your encrypted Cliniko key
OpenAIWrites answers and drafts, turns speech into text and handles voice bookingUnited StatesYes. Questions, drafts, letters, session notes and recordings you send. Patient names are replaced in Ask and the day brief only. Kept for up to 30 days to detect misuse, and not used to train its models.
Google (Maps Routes)Works out drive times for home visitsUnited States and other countriesPatient addresses for home visits. PulseX does not keep them.
Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)Delivers notifications to Android phonesWorldwideNo patient names
Apple (Push Notification service)Delivers notifications to iPhonesWorldwideNo patient names
Google Play and the Apple App StoreTake payment and tell us whether your subscription is activeWorldwideNo patient information

Cliniko is not a subprocessor of PulseX. It is the system where your clinic records live, and you have your own agreement with it.

Transfers outside the UK

For transfers outside the UK we rely on the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses, or on the UK-US data bridge where the company is certified under it.
