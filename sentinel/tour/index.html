How it is held to account

Rules, not vibes

01

Two scores, kept apart

What an agent can cause, and whether that can be reconstructed afterwards, are scored separately.

02

The three-ingredient test

Outside input, private data and an action nobody approves. All three together is an open path.

03

Near misses become proposals

What was learned becomes a control change for a person to approve.
