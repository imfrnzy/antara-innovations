The method

Every AI action passes six questions.

  2. 01

    Identity

    Who is acting? A human, an AI agent, or a shared and delegated login. We establish who, for every action.

  3. 02

    Authority

    What may it do? Least privilege by design, with approval workflows for anything above the limit.

  4. 03

    Context

    What does it use? The data it reads, the outside information it trusts, and how good that data is.

  5. 04

    Action

    What can it cause? Read, write, execute, or trigger other agents. A person stays in the loop where it matters.

  6. 05

    Evidence

    Can we reconstruct it? Inputs, outputs and the approver, in a record that cannot be quietly edited.

  7. 06

    Adaptation

    What should change? Near-misses become control recommendations, and they go back to the start.
