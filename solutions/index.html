Identity

Who is acting?

  • Human user
  • AI agent
  • Shared or delegated identity

Establish and verify identity for every action.

Authority

What is it authorised to do?

  • Task-level authorisation
  • Least privilege by design
  • Approval workflows

Grant the minimum authority required.

Context

What does it use?

  • Data sources and access
  • External information
  • Trust and data quality

Control what informs its decisions, and trust it.

Action & Consequence

What can it cause?

  • Read, write, execute
  • Ability to trigger other agents
  • Human in the loop, where needed

Restrict and monitor real-world impact.

Evidence

  • End-to-end audit logs
  • Decision inputs and outputs
  • Tamper-resistant records

Prove what happened, and why.

Adaptation

  • Control recommendations
  • Evolving threat landscape

Learn from what happened and propose changes.
