Instruments / Bearing

An assessment of your firm's position against the AI regulation that applies to it.

Specify where you operate. The assessment is then scoped to the FCA and SM&CR in the UK, FINMA's guidance in Switzerland, and the EU AI Act. It takes approximately ten minutes.

Three regulatory regimes, one assessment

Many firms answer to more than one regulator. Where the UK, Swiss and EU requirements overlap, they are assessed once, not three times.

Readiness and evidence are assessed separately

Every question distinguishes between what a firm does and what it can demonstrate. Supervisors ask for the latter, and that is where most firms fall short.

No data leaves your browser

Responses are scored locally, against fixed criteria. The assessment does not request customer data, model code or any confidential information.

Included in the free assessment

  • A readiness score for each jurisdiction
  • A ready, partly ready, or not yet ready rating for every obligation
  • The three priority areas identified from your responses
  • A copy you can save and circulate internally

Bearing provides a readiness assessment. It does not constitute legal advice or a compliance certification. Rules current as of September 2026.
