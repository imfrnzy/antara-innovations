Instruments / Ensign

Demonstration version · the deployed Ensign is built for your environment

A label doesn't rescue a claim that's misleading regardless.

Six scenarios test whether you know which disclosure rule actually applies, under the EU AI Act and the ASA's own test. A seventh, written, question is graded on what you'd actually tell a client. About seven minutes.

Escalating counts as right

Some cases are genuinely borderline, even in the regulator's own guidance. Guessing on those is the failure, not the honest answer.

The written answer is graded, not self-marked

Confidence that your reasoning is sound isn't evidence that it would hold up if a client's legal team asked the same question twice.

Nothing confidential is asked

Every scenario is a fictional example. It never asks about your actual clients or campaigns.

Included in the demonstration

  • An overall accuracy score and rating
  • Where your disclosure judgement is strongest and weakest
  • Whether overconfidence shows up in your answers
  • A copy you can save and circulate to your team

This assessment reflects one person's judgement, not your agency's. Circulate the link and compare results.
