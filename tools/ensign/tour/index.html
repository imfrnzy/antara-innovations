Three answers, and escalating is a correct one

Needs a label

Clear-cut cases, an AI system interacting directly with people, or fully synthetic content with no editorial review.

No label needed

Content with genuine human editorial review, or never published beyond an internal pitch.

Check with legal

Cases the guidance itself hasn't fully settled. Guessing confidently here is the failure being tested for.

What you're actually judging

The clear-cut case

A chatbot, or a fully AI-generated image, with nothing telling anyone it isn't human-made.

The genuine exemption

AI-assisted work that had real editorial review, with someone's name actually on it.

The one worth a second opinion

A disclosure that's technically present but buried, or the line between AI-assisted and AI-generated.

Then one question that's actually graded

A client asks you to justify a no-disclosure decision in writing. You write what you'd actually send them, and it's graded against a real standard, not self-marked. "We checked and it's fine" doesn't pass.
