AI regulatory readiness

Bearing

Ready is not the same as able to prove it.

Bearing checks where you stand against SM&CR and Consumer Duty in the UK, FINMA's guidance in Switzerland, and the EU AI Act, in one pass. Tell it whether you're a bank, an insurer or another regulated financial firm, and it asks only what applies to that.

Every obligation comes back as ready, partly, or not yet, never a guess, because the rule for scoring it is fixed before you start.

Human judgement under pressure

Squall

Would your people catch it, or wave it through?

A short simulation for claims, payments or customer teams: a synthetic document, an urgent request that isn't what it looks like. It scores accuracy, confidence and whether process actually held, not whether the detection software is good enough.
