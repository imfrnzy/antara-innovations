AI regulatory readiness

Bearing

Readiness and the ability to demonstrate it are not the same thing.

The Bank of England and FCA's own survey found most firms name three or more people accountable for the same AI use. When a supervisor actually asks, that split is treated the same as no one being accountable at all.

Bearing tests what you could produce today against SM&CR, Consumer Duty, FINMA's guidance and the EU AI Act, not what you believe you know. Questions are graded on what you actually produce.

Human judgement under pressure

Squall

Detection software catches some of it. Your team's judgement is what catches the rest.

Insurers detected more than £233m of suspected fraud last year, much of it built to pass a quick look. The fake CFO message and the doctored claim photo are not hypothetical scenarios written for effect.

Six timed scenarios test whether your team would have caught it, scored for accuracy and for whether confidence matched reality. Wrong and certain is the pattern that actually costs money.
