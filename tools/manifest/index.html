Clinics and practitioners · Manifest

The report your client's insurer needs before treatment can continue.

AXA's standard cover is six sessions. After that, and at every renewal point most insurers set, you write a structured progress report or your client's therapy stops being funded. Manifest drafts it from your own session notes in a couple of minutes, with the clinical numbers calculated the way NHS Talking Therapies calculates them, not estimated by a language model guessing at your intent.

What it actually does

  • You enter session counts, the goals you set, how things have gone, and any outcome-measure scores you already track.
  • Manifest calculates reliable improvement and recovery itself, using the same point-change thresholds NHS Talking Therapies uses for PHQ-9 and GAD-7. That calculation never comes from the AI.
  • It drafts the report in the structure your insurer actually expects. You read it, edit anything, and send it.

What it never asks for

No client name, date of birth, or NHS number, anywhere. You give it a short reference, initials or a case code, nothing that identifies your client if this screen were seen by someone else. You stay the data controller for everything written in the draft.
