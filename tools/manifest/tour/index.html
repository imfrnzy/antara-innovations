Proprietary instrument

MANIFEST

A ship's manifest is the record that has to be accurate before the voyage is allowed to continue.

Six sessions, then a report, or your client's therapy stops.

AXA's standard cover is six sessions. Bupa works the same way. Every extension past that point needs a structured progress report, reviewed before any more sessions are funded. Right now, that report gets written by hand, on your own time, for every client, every few sessions.

The numbers aren't guessed. They're calculated.

Manifest works out reliable improvement and recovery itself, using the same point-change thresholds NHS Talking Therapies uses for PHQ-9 and GAD-7. A drop of 6 or more on the PHQ-9, 4 or more on the GAD-7, that's reliable improvement, by a fixed rule, not an AI's impression of your notes.

The model only writes the sentences around that number. It never gets to decide what the number means.
