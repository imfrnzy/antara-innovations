Instruments / Squall

An assessment of judgement under time pressure.

Six short scenarios, each timed. Decide whether it is genuine, fake or manipulated, or escalate it, in the way the situation would actually require. The assessment takes approximately five minutes.

Escalation is scored as correct

Certain scenarios are deliberately constructed to be genuinely uncertain. Guessing on these is scored as the error, not the honest response.

Confidence is assessed separately

An incorrect answer is one finding. A confidently incorrect answer is a distinct and more significant one.

No confidential information is requested

Every scenario is a fictional example. The assessment does not ask about your actual customers or claims.

Included in the free assessment

  • An overall accuracy score and rating
  • Areas of relative strength and weakness
  • Whether overconfidence is present in your responses
  • A copy you can save and circulate to your team

A single run reflects one individual's judgement, not the team's. Circulate the link and compare results.
