Before you go further

Guessing on the uncertain ones is the actual failure

Not every scenario has a clean answer. Some are built to be genuinely uncertain.

Picking "genuine" or "fake" on one of those isn't confidence, it's a coin toss wearing confidence's clothes. Squall scores escalating as the correct call on exactly those ones, because that's what the real job actually rewards.

Two things get measured, not one

Accuracy

Did the call match what was actually true, including when the true answer was "escalate this".

Calibration

Wrong is one finding. Wrong and marked high confidence is a different, more dangerous one.
