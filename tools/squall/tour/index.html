How it is held to account

Rules, not vibes

01

Wrong and certain is what costs money

Each answer is scored for accuracy and for whether confidence matched reality.

02

Escalating can be the right answer

Where a scenario is genuinely uncertain, guessing is scored as the error.

03

Teams, not individuals

Results are totals by team, with nobody named.

The agent is held to the same questions it asks of everyone else.
