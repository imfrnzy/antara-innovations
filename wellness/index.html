Product · Antara Wellness

The world's first psychological and psychosomatic wellbeing app.

Built on one conviction: people hide things when disclosure feels like it will be analysed against them before it helps them. So Antara was designed to make people feel understood, not assessed.

Not how to use your phone less

Most wellbeing products try to pull people away from technology. Antara starts from the opposite premise, that the relationship between people and their tools has already been rewired, and the harder, more useful problem is building something that works with that shape rather than pretending it isn't there.
