Product · Antara Wellness

The world's first psychological and psychosomatic wellbeing app.

Built on one conviction: people hide things when disclosure feels like it will be analysed against them before it helps them.

So Antara was designed to make people feel understood, not assessed.

Visit antara.icu →

The idea

Not how to use your phone less

Most wellbeing products try to pull people away from technology.

Antara starts from the opposite premise: the relationship between people and their tools has already been rewired. The harder, more useful problem is building something that works with that shape rather than pretending it isn't there.
